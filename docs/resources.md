# Resources

Pre-reading and reference material for Consentric participants. None of this is required before the event — the workshops will cover everything you need. But if you want a head start, these are good places to look.

## Ethereum Basics

- **[Ethereum.org — Learn Hub](https://ethereum.org/en/learn/)** — The official starting point. Covers what Ethereum is, how it works, and why it exists.
- **[Ethereum.org — Developers](https://ethereum.org/en/developers/)** — Developer-focused docs, tutorials, and tooling guides.
- **[What is Ethereum? (Whiteboard Crypto)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jxLkbJozKbY)** — A plain-language video explainer if you prefer watching over reading.

## Solidity & Smart Contracts

- **[Solidity Documentation](https://docs.soliditylang.org/)** — The official language docs. Start with the "Introduction to Smart Contracts" section.
- **[CryptoZombies](https://cryptozombies.io/)** — Interactive Solidity tutorial. Gamified, beginner-friendly, and you'll have a working project by the end.
- **[Solidity by Example](https://solidity-by-example.org/)** — Short, focused code examples for common patterns.

## Development Tools

- **[Hardhat Documentation](https://hardhat.org/docs)** — The development framework used in this event's starter kits. Covers setup, compiling, testing, and deploying contracts.
- **[MetaMask](https://metamask.io/)** — The browser wallet you'll use during Workshop I. Install it before the event if you can.
- **[Remix IDE](https://remix.ethereum.org/)** — Browser-based Solidity editor. Good for quick experiments without any local setup.

## Selective Disclosure & Zero-Knowledge Basics

- **[What are Zero-Knowledge Proofs? (Ethereum.org)](https://ethereum.org/en/zero-knowledge-proofs/)** — Plain-language overview of ZK proofs and why they matter for privacy.
- **[Zero Knowledge Proofs — Computerphile (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HUs1bH85X9I)** — A clear, visual explanation of how ZK proofs work conceptually.
- **[Introduction to ZK-SNARKs (z.cash)](https://z.cash/learn/what-are-zk-snarks/)** — Goes a level deeper into one specific type of ZK proof. Optional but interesting.

## Health Records & Indian Context

- **[Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)](https://abdm.gov.in/)** — India's national digital health infrastructure program. This is the real-world system your hackathon projects relate to.
- **[ABDM Sandbox](https://sandbox.abdm.gov.in/)** — Developer sandbox for the ABDM APIs. Not required for this event, but useful context for what India is building.
- **[National Health Authority — About](https://nha.gov.in/)** — The government body behind ABDM and Ayushman Bharat.

## General Blockchain & Privacy

- **[Why Decentralization Matters (Chris Dixon)](https://onereader.medium.com/why-decentralization-matters-5e3f79f7638e)** — A widely-read essay on why decentralized systems matter, not specific to health but directly relevant.
- **[The Meaning of Decentralization (Vitalik Buterin)](https://medium.com/@VitalikButerin/the-meaning-of-decentralization-a0c92b76a274)** — Ethereum's co-founder on what "decentralized" actually means in practice.
