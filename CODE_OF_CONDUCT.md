# Code of Conduct

## The Short Version

Be respectful. Be kind. Don't be a jerk.

This is a beginner-friendly hackathon. Many participants will be writing their first line of Solidity, making their first transaction, or opening their first pull request. That's the whole point. Don't make anyone feel stupid for not knowing something yet.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat everyone with respect and consideration
- Be welcoming to newcomers — help them if you can, or point them to someone who can
- Give constructive feedback, not dismissive criticism
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Focus on what's best for learning and building, not for ego

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment of any kind — verbal, physical, or online
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Intimidation, stalking, or unwelcome attention
- Dismissing or belittling someone's questions or skill level
- Any conduct that would make a reasonable person feel unsafe or unwelcome

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all event spaces — the venue, the repo, any group chats or channels created for the event, and any interactions between participants during and around the event.

## Reporting Issues

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable or that you think violates this code of conduct:

1. **In person:** Find any event organizer — they'll be identifiable at the venue. They'll handle it discreetly.
2. **Online:** Reach out via DM on [Instagram](https://instagram.com/mew3india) or [X](https://x.com/Mew3india).

All reports will be handled with discretion. We're here to make sure everyone can focus on building, not on dealing with bad behavior.

## Enforcement

Organizers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warning the offender, asking them to leave the event, or removing their contributions from the repository.
